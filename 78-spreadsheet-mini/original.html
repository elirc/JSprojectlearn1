Mini spreadsheet

Type a number, or a formula like =A1+B2*2. The small grey number under each cell is what the sheet thinks the cell is worth.

Watch A1. A1 is =B1+1, B1 is =C1*2, C1 is 5, so A1 should be 11. It isn't. Click into any cell, type a space, delete it — and watch A1 crawl one step closer each time. D1 and E1 refer to each other and grow forever.
